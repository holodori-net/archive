Thank you as always for playing hololive Dreams.

Thank you very much for all the responses to our September survey.
Based on your feedback, we are working on improvements to make the game easier to play, as well as developing new content.
In this post, we'd like to share our planned improvements to training features and the Holomem Board, which received a particularly large amount of feedback, along with information on upcoming updates.



◆Improvements Planned for the Late-October Update

・Players will be able to claim Achievement Rewards all at once.
・Allow players to switch between the Achievs screen and Holomem Board for the same talent.
・Players will be able to move to the corresponding talent's Holomem Board from the Member Details Screen.



◆Improvements Being Considered for November and Beyond

・Players will be able to Bloom multiple Members at once.
・A preset feature for saving Holomem Board settings
・The number of unlocked Holomem Board nodes will be displayed for each talent.
・Players will be able to freely select which nodes to unlock on the Holomem Board.
Note: Only nodes that are adjacent and available to unlock can be added to your selection.


We are also considering other improvements based on the feedback we have received.
We will announce them as they become ready.



◆Upcoming Updates

Taking into account the feedback on the latest new Area, we will continue to focus on developing new Areas and Minigames.
We plan to introduce new gameplay experiences every few months.

In addition, based on your survey feedback, we are preparing new content that will allow players to take their time improving their Rhythm Game scores outside of limited-time events.
We hope you'll enjoy gradually raising your score at your own pace while fine-tuning your training and deck builds.

We are also preparing updates that expand the ways you can play the rhythm game.
Planned features include a party mode-like feature centered on Private Rooms where everyone can have fun together, as well as features that let you master the rhythm game with greater precision.
Major feature additions to Create Charts are also planned.
We hope these will allow you to enjoy the rhythm game in a wide variety of ways.
Further details and implementation schedules will be shared in future announcements, so please stay tuned.

By continuing to introduce new areas and Minigames while expanding Rhythm Game features, we aim to offer more ways to play.
Our goal is to create a game where everyone can choose how they want to play based on their preferences and continue enjoying the game in their own way.



◆Upcoming Card Illustrations

Since card illustrations with a swimsuit motif have appeared several times in a row, we'd like to share our plans going forward.
Our policy is to deliver card illustrations featuring a variety of motifs while also taking the seasons into account.
Due to adjustments in our development and production schedules, swimsuit motifs have continued for a while, but the "Blissful Seaside Days Gacha" will be the last of them for now.
After that, we plan to feature different motifs, so please look forward to what's to come.


We will continue to use your feedback to make the game easier to play and to develop new content.
We also have a holodori Official Stream planned for late October, so please look forward to it.


Thank you for your continued support of hololive Dreams.
