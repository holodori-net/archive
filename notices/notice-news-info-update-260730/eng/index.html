◆Minigame “Collect Cooking” Change

We’ve implemented the following adjustment to address an issue that made it possible to achieve scores that couldn’t be reached through normal gameplay by performing specific actions.


  • Pausing Disabled
  • In-game time will now continue to pass even while the app is in the background while playing.


In addition, due to this issue, we plan to reset the rankings for “Collect Cooking.”

For details, please see the notice “Currently confirmed issues.”


◆Streamer Mode Added

Streamer Mode has been added to help prevent music rights issues when sharing gameplay content.

When Streamer Mode is enabled, the volume for specific songs will be set to 0.

Go to [Settings] > [System] to configure [Streamer Mode].



◆Bug Fixes

The following issues have been resolved.

  • Under certain cirumstances, players may be unable to progress after initiating a game of Mega Circuit.
  • Under certain cirumstances, players may be unable to progress during a game of Pokajan!.
  • If you link your account to a different device before clearing Main Story Chapter 1 Episode 1 "The Beginning of A Dream", there may be cases were your player name is reset and left blank.
  • Fixed various minor issues.
