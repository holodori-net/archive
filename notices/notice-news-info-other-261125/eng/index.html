Thank you for playing hololive Dreams.
We would like to share our plans for introducing new cards starting from late November, along with some of the content coming in the Ver. 1.3.0 update.



◆New Cards from Late November Onward

To ensure that all of our players can enjoy the game,
we aim to keep the number of cards available for each talent equal.


We originally intended to release one new card for every talent before introducing a second card.
However, the timing of the game's release caused our original card release schedule to shift.
Starting in late November, in order to release the seasonal cards we had prepared during their intended seasons,
some talents' second cards will arrive before other talents' first ones.


We are working toward releasing the first card for every talent by January 2027.
We apologize for keeping everyone waiting for your favorite talent's card, and appreciate your patience.


While this will temporarily create a difference in the number of cards for each talent, we will work hard to avoid a similar situation in the future wherever possible.
We will do our best to continue delivering cards that showcase each talent's unique appeal.



◆New Content: Challenge Live

Challenge Live puts your Members' training and your team building skills to the test as you work toward clearing all 100 stages.


However, this mode is not designed with the expectation that players will clear every floor at launch.
We envision players taking on the challenge over time as they train their Members, including future Holomem Board expansions.
We encourage players to start by seeing how far you can get, and when you've trained your Members further or want to try a new team, try taking on the next floors.


Rewards will include new materials used to unlock additional Nodes on Holomem Boards, as well as training items and Diamonds.
We hope everyone will enjoy progressing at their own pace, seeing the Members' growth pay off, and achieving new goals.



◆Holomem Board Expansions and Improvements

We are adding new Nodes to Holomem Boards so players can aim for even higher scores through Member training and creative team building.
Unlocking these new Nodes requires "Star Cubes," a training material which can be obtained from the Challenge Live.
We plan on expanding Holomem Boards gradually, offering more ways to enjoy training members and experimenting with different teams.


We will also continue to gradually improve Holomem Board usability.
This update also adds a Preset feature that lets players save and change configurations for each holomem.
Further improvements are planned, including a way to change configurations for an entire Unit all at once.



Regarding the Ver. 1.3.0 Update:
This update also includes a new Minigame that can be enjoyed in both Solo and Multiplayer, and more content.
We will continue to treat every talent with care as we expand Member training methods, improve usability, and add new content.
Our goal is to create a game where players can choose how they want to play and enjoy the game in their own way.


An official stream is also planned for late November, so please look forward to it.
We appreciate your continued support for hololive Dreams.
