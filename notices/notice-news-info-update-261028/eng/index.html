◆Improvements and updates to Achievements and the Holomem Board

  • Achievement rewards can now be claimed by category.
  • If claiming achievement rewards unlocks more rewards, the details screen will remain open.
  • You can now go to a holomem’s Holomem Board from the Achievement screen.
  • You can now go to a holomem’s Achievement screen from their Holomem Board.
  • You can now go to a holomem’s Holomem Board from the Member Details screen.



◆Other Updates

  • It is now possible to see whether it was yourself or another player who disconnected in the Live Together Result Screen.
  • In the Song Selection Screen, after the number of displayed songs becomes zero due to filtering, the previously selected song will now be retained when the list is displayed again.
  • When moving to the Event Exchange, it has been improved so that the Exchange for the relevant Chapter is selected by default. Outside of the event period, after confirming the guidance, you will now be taken to the Event Portal screen.
  • Improved the stability and behavior when closing and reopening Live Screens that include MVs.
  • Google Play Games linking is now supported.
