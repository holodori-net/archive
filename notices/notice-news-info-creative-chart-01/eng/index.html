Thank you for using our service.

This is the hololive Dreams management team.



In order to maintain a welcoming environment where everyone can create and enjoy original charts, we regularly review charts shared by the community.


During the review process, we identified a number of charts which were not suitable for the game and have removed them.



Please be aware that charts falling under the following categories may be removed at the discretion of the team going forward.

  • Charts that may infringe on the rights of third parties, such as those that reproduce or imitate charts from other titles.
    *Excluding content where reuse has been authorized by the rights holder.
  • Charts that exploit bugs or game mechanics.
  • Any other charts that the team deems inappropriate, including those containing offensive or otherwise unsuitable content.

Furthermore, depending on the nature and severity of the violation, we may take actions that go beyond chart removal, such as account suspension.



Thank you for your understanding and cooperation in helping maintain a welcoming environment where everyone can enjoy creating and sharing charts.



We hope that you continue to enjoy hololive Dreams.
