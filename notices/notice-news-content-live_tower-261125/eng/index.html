◆Challenge Live Release

2026-11-25 11:00:00~



◆Entry Conditions

Clear Area Quest "To the Stage of Dreams! Mission Start."



* The Area Quest "To the Stage of Dreams! Mission Start" unlocks by completing Main Quest Chapter 3 "Water Stage."



New mode: Challenge Live



Challenge Live is a mode where you take on Set Pieces for each Stage and aim to reach the Target Score.

If you reach the Target Score, the Stage is cleared, you earn rewards, and move on to the next Stage.

You can progress up to 100 Stages.


If you include different Bonus Members set for each Stage in your formation, Bonus Effects will be applied and your Unit Score will increase.



Rewards



By clearing specific Stages, you can obtain new items such as Red Star Cubes and Achievement Badges.

* Achievement Badges can be obtained from Achievs.


Red Star Cubes can be used to unlock Nodes on Holomem Boards added in the Ver. 1.3.0 update.

For details, please see the "Holomem Board Update" section in the Ver. 1.3.0 Update announcement.
