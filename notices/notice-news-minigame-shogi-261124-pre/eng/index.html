New Minigame: Puka Puka Fight



Puka Puka Fight is a Minigame where you aim to win by knocking the opponent's Leader into the water using your holomems!

If you knock opposing holomems other than the Leader into the water, you can recruit them to your team!

You can face NPCs in Solo Play and other players in Multiplayer.



Victory Conditions



◆Knock the opponent’s Leader into the water

Knock the opponent's Leader (the holomem with the crown) into the water to win!



◆Try

You can also win by moving your Leader to a platform in the rightmost column.

If your Leader can't be knocked into the water on the next turn after reaching the rightmost column, you win!



Team Formation



The direction each holomem can move depends on its hat!

There are four kinds of movement patterns: Leader (Crown), Dolphin, Whale, and Turtle (Green).



◆Movements

  • Leader: 1 Tile in any direction.
  • Dolphin: 1 Tile in the four cardinal directions.
  • Whale: 1 Tile in the four diagonal directions.
  • Turtle (Green): 1 Tile forward.
  • Turtle (Gold): 1 Tile in any direction, except diagonally backward.



Actions During Your Turn



You and your opponent take turns moving holomems.

  • Moving: Select one of your holomems, then choose an available platform to move them to.
  • Placing Allies: Select a recruited holomem, then place them on an empty platform.


* If you move onto a platform occupied by an opposing holomem, you'll knock them into the water.



Turtle Power-ups



When a Turtle (Green) holomem moves to a platform in the rightmost column, it powers up into a Turtle (Gold)!

This powered-up turtle can move 1 Tile in any direction, except the two diagonal directions behind it.


* A recruited Turtle (Green) will not power up if placed directly on a platform in the rightmost column.

* A powered-up turtle will go back to its original form if it's knocked into the water.



Solo Play



Solo Play offers two modes: NORMAL and CHALLENGE.



◆NORMAL

This is a mode where you take on 30 different stages.

Defeat the holomems in each stage to advance.



◆CHALLENGE

You can unlock this mode by completing all NORMAL stages.

The holomems' positions change with each attempt, letting you enjoy battles against opponents of various skill levels.



Undo




In Solo Play you can use Undo.

If you press Undo, you can return to your previous turn.


* Can't be used in Multiplayer.



Rewards




You can earn Soda Pop Coins and new fishing bait as rewards.



◆NORMAL Rewards

Earn clear rewards the first time you clear each stage in NORMAL Mode.


* You will not gain rewards when replaying a cleared stage.



◆CHALLENGE and Multiplayer Rewards

In CHALLENGE and Multiplayer modes, you can earn rewards whether you win or lose.


* Multiplayer Private Rooms give no rewards.



◆Minigame Boost

In NORMAL and CHALLENGE modes, the amount of Minigame Boosts used is fixed.

However, in Multiplayer you can choose how many Minigame Boosts to use.

In Multiplayer, the more Minigame Boosts you use, the more rewards you can earn.



Important Notes

  • The duration and contents may change without warning.
  • The perks of HoloPassport or Holomem Boards do not affect NORMAL mode's clear rewards.
