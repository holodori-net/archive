New Area “Soda Pop Lagoon" Summary



Soda Pop Lagoon is a new area consisting of a vast ocean and multiple islands.


You can travel around the area by boat.


As you progress through the story, the sea areas you can explore will expand.



New feature “Fishing" added!



"Fishing" is a new feature that lets you catch fish at fishing spots within the new area.


The types of fish you can catch vary depending on the bait, spot, and time of day.


Catch fish to earn rewards and caught fish will be recorded in the Fish Guide.

Rewards can be earned based on the type of fish you catch.


* New feature “Fishing" is unlocked by progressing through Main Quests Chapter 3 “Soda Pop Lagoon".



New Minigame “Splash Ball" has been added!



Splash Ball is a Minigame where three teams of three players battle it out.


You can score points against the opposing teams by hitting the ball into their goal while riding a boat.


Rewards can be earned based on your personal score and team ranking.


* New Minigame “Splash Ball" is unlocked by progressing through Main Quests Chapter 3 “Soda Pop Lagoon".
* For Multiplayer Private Rooms, you can play it by clearing Main Quest Chapter 1 Episode 4 "Mythical Connection".
