Since the update to Ver.1.3.0, multiple issues have occurred in Live, the Holomem Board, and other areas of the game.


We deeply apologize for once again causing inconvenience and disruption to our players,
as well as for not sufficiently explaining our response to these issues during the ongoing event.


We would like to explain the circumstances behind these issues, the current status of our investigation, and our planned response.


◆ Background of the Multiple Issues

Following the update to Ver.1.3.0, several issues related to the update have occurred.


Our investigation has also confirmed that, during the development of the update, some fixes implemented in previous versions were not properly carried over to the new version.
We deeply apologize for the inconvenience caused by both newly introduced issues and the recurrence of issues that had previously been fixed.


The following are the main issues newly introduced in Ver.1.3.0, as well as those confirmed to have recurred.
The timing of fixes will vary depending on the issue. We will address them as soon as fixes are ready and continue working to resolve them as quickly as possible.


▽ Issues Scheduled to Be Fixed by Next Week

  • In "Live Together", Holomem Board effects with "Song Performer" and "Group Songs" conditions do not activate.
  • When loading a Holomem Board preset, nodes whose unlock status remains unchanged are also reset, contrary to the intended behavior, causing more Hologold to be consumed than necessary.
  • When attempting to play "Challenge Live" with no Auto Tickets remaining, an error occurs, causing players to be returned to the title screen.


▽ Issues That Will Require More Time to Fix

  • On some Android devices, the game may temporarily become unstable during Live gameplay.
  • In "Live Together", if the difficulty selection screen times out, the difficulty is set to "Normal" and the Live Boost consumption amount is set to 0.

*We have received various feedback regarding the waiting time before a Live begins and are considering improvements for future updates.


*Please check the in-game notice for details on these and other issues.


◆ Response Regarding the Ongoing Event

We would once again like to apologize for the inconvenience caused by the issue that occurred yesterday in "Live Together", which affects scores.


As fixing this issue will require additional time and cannot be completed during the event period, we have decided to prioritize preserving the scores and results players have already achieved.
For these reasons, we have decided to continue the event while maintaining the current rankings, without conducting emergency maintenance or resetting the rankings.


We sincerely apologize for not sufficiently explaining these circumstances in yesterday's announcement.
This issue is scheduled to be fixed in an update after the event ends.


◆ Apology and Compensation

As an apology for the impact on the event and the series of issues, we will distribute the following items to all players.


  • Red Diamonds ×2,500
  • Live Boost Drinks ×100
  • Auto Tickets ×100
  • Hologold ×1,500,000


In addition to the items listed above, we also plan to compensate players for any excess Hologold consumed due to the issue that occurs when loading Holomem Board presets.
This compensation is scheduled to be distributed after the issue has been fixed.


We sincerely apologize for the continued issues and the inconvenience and disruption they have caused to everyone who has been looking forward to enjoying Live and participating in events.
